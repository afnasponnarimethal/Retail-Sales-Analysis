## Executive Data Analysis Project | 2020-2024

This project transforms a synthetic order-level retail dataset into a clean, validated, and analysis-ready dataset. The workflow covers data understanding, cleaning, feature engineering, non-visual exploratory analysis, visual analysis, management interpretation, validation, and export.

> **Important:** The dataset is synthetic. The results demonstrate an analytical workflow and should not be interpreted as audited organizational performance.

# Phase 2A: Data Cleaning

## Step 7: Remove exact duplicates

The notebook:

## Step 8: Remove the completely empty row

The record count changes from 4,201 to 4,200.

## Step 9: Standardize order dates

## Step 10: Standardize gender classifications


## Step 11: Standardize order statuses


## Step 12: Clean customer age

The notebook applies the following rule:

```text

```

Processing steps:

1. Convert invalid ages to missing values.
2. Calculate the valid median age.
3. Fill missing and invalid ages with the median.
4. Validate the final age distribution.

## Step 13: Clean order quantity

Processing steps:

1. Identify missing, zero, and negative quantities.
2. Convert invalid quantities to missing values.
3. Fill missing and invalid quantities using the valid median.
4. Review the resulting distribution.

Confirmed extreme quantity errors are handled separately during outlier treatment.

## Step 14: Clean shipping cost

Processing steps:

1. Identify negative and missing shipping costs.
2. Convert negative values to missing values.
3. Fill missing and invalid shipping costs using the valid median.
4. Validate that final shipping costs are non-negative.

## Step 15: Clean shipping duration

Processing steps:

1. Identify missing, zero, and negative shipping durations.
2. Convert invalid durations to missing values.
3. Fill missing and invalid values using the valid median.
4. Validate the resulting distribution.

Confirmed extreme duration errors are handled separately during outlier treatment.

## Step 16: Treat missing discounts

Missing `discount_pct` values are interpreted as no discount and replaced with:

```text
0
```

The field is validated to remain between 0 and 1.

## Step 17: Process customer satisfaction

The notebook:

1. Converts satisfaction to numeric values.
2. Retains only valid ratings from 1 to 5.
3. Keeps missing ratings as missing instead of imputing a score.
4. Creates `rating_status` with:

```text
Rated
Not Rated
```

This preserves feedback integrity and allows rating coverage to be reported beside satisfaction averages.

## Step 18: Remove confirmed quantity errors

The IQR review identifies eight extreme quantity records above the accepted operational range.

These records are treated as confirmed data-entry errors and removed.

The record count changes from 4,200 to 4,192.

## Step 19: Remove confirmed shipping-duration errors

The IQR review identifies six extreme shipping durations above the accepted operational range.

These records are treated as confirmed data-entry errors and removed.

The final record count changes from 4,192 to 4,186.

## 8. Data-Cleaning Assumptions

The project applies the following assumptions:

- Customer ages below 15 or above 80 are invalid.
- Missing and invalid ages are filled using the valid median.
- Missing and invalid quantities are filled using the valid median before confirmed extreme errors are removed.
- Negative shipping costs are invalid and replaced using the valid median.
- Missing or non-positive shipping durations are replaced using the valid median before confirmed extreme errors are removed.
- Missing discounts indicate no discount and are replaced with zero.
- Missing satisfaction scores are retained and classified as Not Rated.
- Statistical outliers are not automatically removed.
- High-value prices, sales, and profit are retained when logically plausible.
- Only confirmed extreme quantity and shipping-duration errors are removed.

## 9. Project Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and does not represent audited organizational performance.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causality.
- Recorded sales and profit are analyzed as supplied by the dataset.
- Satisfaction averages exclude orders without a valid rating.
- Rating coverage is reported to show feedback completeness.
- Status-change timestamps are unavailable, so formal order-aging analysis cannot be performed.
- Return reasons and cancellation reasons are unavailable.
- Promised delivery dates and campaign identifiers are unavailable.
- City coordinates are approximate visualization anchors and do not represent customer addresses or delivery routes.
- Status-level financial values are not treated as accounting-recognized revenue.
